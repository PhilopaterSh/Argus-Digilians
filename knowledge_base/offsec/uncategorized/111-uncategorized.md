## Scenario

Is it worth spending time on Vulnerability Disclosure Programs (VDPs) that don't pay?

## Analysis / Response

In my opinion yes, but with limits. VDPs are a great way to practise new research and sharpen your skills, because the end goal is building relationships and becoming a better hacker (while helping secure the internet). A few reasons to spend time in a VDP: to practise a new technique, to chase some cool swag, or just for a challenge on a Sunday afternoon.

The caution: know your limits and don't burn out giving a company a complete free pentest. Companies want talent, so even if they don't pay, demonstrating your skills can put you at the top of their invite list if they later upgrade the VDP to a paying program. Always weigh your risk-vs-reward ratio when playing in VDPs - give enough to show value and learn, but don't exhaust yourself for free.
